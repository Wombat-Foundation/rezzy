## 1. Executive Summary & Public-Safety Threat Model

`rezzy` is a high-performance, `#![no_std]`-compatible (`alloc`) Rust implementation of Matrix protocol state resolution (V1, V2/[MSC1693](https://spec.matrix.org/proposals/), V2.1/[MSC4297](https://matrix.org/docs/spec-guides/state-res-2.1/), experimental V2.1.1, and experimental V2.2/[MSC4242](https://spec.matrix.org/proposals/)), paired with `rezzy-json` (canonical JSON), `rezzy-recon` ([MSC4521](https://matrix.org/blog/2026/07/31/this-week-in-matrix-2026-07-31/) $\text{GF}(2^{64})$ PinSketch set reconciliation and [MSC4511](https://matrix.org/blog/2026/07/24/this-week-in-matrix-2026-07-24/) leaf-compressed causal tries), and `rezzy-cli`.

In public-safety, emergency response, defense, or government command-and-control deployments of Matrix, **state resolution is the distributed access-control, permission enforcement, and revocation kernel**. Any flaw in state resolution or event ingestion causes one of three operational failures:
1. **Federation Split-Brain (Consensus Divergence):** Participating emergency dispatch homeservers compute different resolved states for the same incident room, silently dropping responder messages or isolating field units.
2. **State Reset / Revocation Bypass ([CVE-2025-49090](https://matrix.org/blog/2025/08/project-hydra-improving-state-res/)):** Compromised or banned accounts retain administrative privileges or re-enter locked-down incident rooms.
3. **Asymmetric Denial of Service (Liveness Collapse):** Malicious or malfunctioning federation peers inject pathological DAG structures, colliding algebraic sketches, or wide frontiers that exhaust CPU or RAM during network partition recovery.

## 3. Prioritized Remediation Roadmap (Stage B Standalone Tracks `13`–`18`)

1. **P0 — Make `MSC4307` Rule 2.5 Unconditionally Fail-Closed (`src/auth/`, `spec_audit.md`):** Replace `room_id: Option<RoomId>` on `LeanEvent` (or require a room token in `check_auth_chain`) so foreign-room `auth_events` are always rejected.
2. **P0 — Move Retired CDO Out of Production `src/` (`src/resolve/cdo.rs`, `cdo_soundness_anomalies.md`):** Relocate `cdo.rs` into `tests/` so downstream integrators cannot invoke unsound pre-filtering.
3. **P0 — Seal Permissive Canonical JSON & Lossy `as_f64` (`rezzy-json`, `json_numeric_limits.md`):** Gate `write_raw_canonical_filtered` behind a test-only flag so production hashing/signing strictly enforces `write_raw_canonical_filtered_strict`, and fail closed on integers $>2^{53}$.
4. **P0 — Prevent Mixed-Federation Split-Brain (`rezzy_msc4242_security_comparison.md`, Issue #2437):** Do not mix V2.1.1 (`SEND01` divergence) or ZIP-215 `ed25519-consensus` verification with strict RFC 8032 `ed25519-dalek` / Stock V2.1 homeservers in the same room version.
5. **P1 — Add E2E Adversarial Bucket-Split Tests & Pre-Auth Validation (`tech_debt.md`, `homeserver_utilities.md`):** Add the missing multi-round capacity-then-depth retry test to `test_reconcile_e2e.rs` and implement `src/auth/validate.rs` + `src/dag/fork.rs`.
6. **P1 — Harden Hash Tables & Large-Room Interning (`perf_audits.md`, `tech_debt.md`):** Replace unseeded `foldhash` on network-supplied string keys with CSPRNG-seeded SipHash or `DenseIndex<T, u32>`, and unblock `u32` arena interning (`InternId`) to eliminate the +3% to +16% atomic contention penalty at 5,000+ room members.
