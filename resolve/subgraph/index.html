Conflicted subgraph extraction for MSC4297 (V2.1+).

When resolving state under V2.1+, the algorithm needs the conflicted subgraph — the intersection of events reachable backwards (ancestors) and forwards (descendants) from the conflicted set through the auth DAG.

This ensures that only events causally relevant to the conflict are considered, preventing unrelated auth chain history from influencing the outcome.
