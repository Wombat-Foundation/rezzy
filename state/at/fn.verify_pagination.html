Expand description

Verifies that a sequence of pagination pages respects DAG ordering invariants:

  1. No duplicates — each event ID appears on at most one page.
  2. Topological monotonicity — if event A is an ancestor of B, then A must not appear on an earlier page than B (in backward pagination, descendants come first).

This is a pure property checker. Feed it the actual pages from your paginator; any violation is a bug in the storage/pagination layer.

Completeness (every reachable event present) is intentionally NOT checked — pagination may stop at room creation, budget limits, or ACL boundaries.

§Returns

A Vec of violations. Empty means the pages are well-formed.
